What we offer

Four paths to a longer life

Each programme is a gentle architecture of movement, breath, silence and nourishment — designed for small groups and shaped around the season, the landscape and the people who come.

Yoga & Breathwork

Morning practice on the shore and in mountain clearings: asana, pranayama and restorative sequences guided by experienced teachers.

Meditation & Inner Silence

Guided contemplation, silent walks and sound healing in monasteries, canyons and lakesides — the deep quiet that restores the nervous system.

Active Journeys

Hiking in Durmitor and Prokletije, rafting the Tara canyon, sea kayaking and long coastal walks — movement as medicine.

Detox & Seasonal Cuisine

Mediterranean and mountain nutrition: olive oil, wild herbs, figs, freshwater fish and seasonal produce that supports vitality.

The land

Montenegro — a country of four climates

In a single day one can practice on a mountain lake, cross a high pass, and bathe in the Adriatic. The landscape itself is the teacher.
